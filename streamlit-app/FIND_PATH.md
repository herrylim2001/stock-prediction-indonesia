### ⚠️ PENTING:

1. **Branch HARUS:** `claude/video-livestream-prototype-UZbhE`
   - BUKAN `main`
   - BUKAN `master`

2. **Path HARUS:** `streamlit-app/streamlit_app.py`
   - BUKAN `streamlit_app.py` (tanpa folder)
   - BUKAN `streamlit-app/` (tanpa filename)

3. **Repository format:**
   - Format: `username/repo-name`
   - Contoh: `herrylim2001/stock-prediction-indonesia`
